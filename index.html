Frequently asked

Questions we hear every week

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on the size of the property, how often you would like us to come and what the space needs. That is why we do not publish fixed prices: instead we give you a free written estimate with no obligation, so you know the exact price before we start.

Will I have the same cleaners every time?

Yes. Recurring clients keep the same crew, on the same day and in the same time slot. It matters to us as much as it matters to you — a team that already knows your home does a better job.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many of our clients give us a key or a door code, which we handle under a written access protocol. Our staff is legally employed and covered by workers' compensation and liability insurance.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring everything needed. If you prefer that we use your own products — for allergies, pets or a particular surface — simply let us know and we will.

Which areas do you serve?

Naples, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Marco Island, Estero, Golden Gate Estates and Punta Gorda. If you are just outside those areas, call us — we often make exceptions.

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. You can call +1 (239) 285-6103, send a text message, or email us at any time and we will reply during business hours.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry workers' compensation for our team and general liability insurance for your property. We are happy to provide a certificate of insurance on request.
